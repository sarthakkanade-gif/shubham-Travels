Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking with us

How do I book my first cab?
You can book by filling the form above, clicking the WhatsApp button or calling us directly at +91 98221 19033. We'll confirm your booking instantly.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept Cash, UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Direct Bank Transfer and Debit/Credit Cards — whichever is convenient for you.
How long does it take to confirm the cab?
Booking is confirmed instantly. Driver's name, contact and vehicle details are sent via WhatsApp and SMS 1 hour before your pickup time.
Can I cancel my booking?
Yes, cancellations are accepted. Please contact us at least 2 hours before the pickup time. Our executive will guide you through the cancellation process.
Are your drivers verified and safe?
Yes. All our drivers are registered, background-checked and have valid commercial driving licenses. Your safety is our top priority.
Do you provide outstation cabs for multiple days?
Absolutely! We offer multi-day outstation cab rentals across Maharashtra, Goa, Gujarat and other states. Just let us know your itinerary.
